SaveWise

A free browser extension that helps you tell whether a price is good, and lets you know when it drops.

Add to Chrome Add to Edge

What it does

  • Price history: see the prices you've seen for a product and whether today's price is the lowest you've seen.
  • Price-drop alerts: tap "Watch" and get a notification when the price falls. Set a target price if you like.
  • Compare prices: one tap searches the same product on other stores.
  • Coupon tester: at checkout, one click tests a list of codes and keeps the best one. Nothing runs until you click.
  • Share a deal: send a price drop to WhatsApp or X.
  • Weekly savings summary (optional) and backup of your history.
  • Available in English, Español, Português, Deutsch, Français and हिन्दी.

How SaveWise is funded

SaveWise is free. On some stores it can open links through an affiliate program, so a store may pay us a commission if you buy. This never changes your price. It happens only when you click a SaveWise button (for example a Compare button), or click a price-drop alert or shared deal while "Use our affiliate link" is on in Settings. You can turn it off any time.

Privacy Policy

What SaveWise stores: prices you view or choose to watch, product titles and addresses, your watchlist and your settings (including language). All of it stays on your device in browser storage. We have no server and receive none of it.

Websites SaveWise reads: only the page you open SaveWise on, and stores you explicitly allow for automatic tracking or alerts. For watched products, SaveWise fetches the product page (without cookies) about every 6 hours to read its price.

Coupon list: when you test coupons, SaveWise may download a small text file of coupon codes from this website. As with any web request, the host can see your IP address. The file contains only codes, never code or links.

Compare prices and Share: nothing is sent until you click. Clicking a Compare button opens that store's search page with the product title as the search term (through an affiliate network where applicable). Sharing opens WhatsApp or X with a message you can see first; the message includes a disclosure when it contains an affiliate link.

Weekly summary and alerts: computed on your device from prices stored on your device. Notifications are optional and only requested when you turn them on.

Third parties: if you click a SaveWise affiliate link, you go through an affiliate network (for example Skimlinks) or directly to a partner brand. They may set cookies and see that you came from SaveWise, and the store may pay us a commission. Your price doesn't change. You can turn this off in Settings.

What we don't do: no analytics, no ads, no selling data, no tracking across sites.

Deleting data: use "Clear price history" in the popup, "Stop watching" in Settings, or uninstall the extension.
